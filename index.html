I build Lean 4 proof-checking pipelines, input-screening libraries, and human-in-the-loop back-office software. Most of the notes here examine what breaks when untrusted files, inbound mail, or AI-authored code cross into compilers, spreadsheets, or external model APIs without an explicit check in front of them.

All adversarial test cases referenced below—checker bypass modules, disguised binary headers, CSV formula strings, and hidden HTML/Unicode markers—are synthetic regression fixtures run locally against my own code.

  • 2026-11-16 Input Screening LLM Triage

    Screening Untrusted Office Inputs Before LLM Triage

    Design and known limits of inbox-guard, a zero-dependency Node.js library that screens inbound business emails, attachments, and CSV exports before model calls or human review. Covers leading magic-byte checks (MZ/ELF/Mach-O renamed to .pdf), Unicode U+202E and Windows/SMB filename spoofing, accounting-safe OWASP CSV neutralization and RFC-4180 serialization, Plane-14 Unicode Tag (U+E0000..U+E007F) & entity-decoded CSS-hidden text detection, From display-name spoofing, and single-pass session-scoped PII tokenization.
