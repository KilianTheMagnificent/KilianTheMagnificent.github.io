I build high-assurance verifiers, defensive input-screening pipelines, and deterministic human-in-the-loop workflow software. My security and verification work centers on a simple architectural premise: untrusted inputs and language-model outputs should cross explicit, machine-checked boundaries before reaching compilers, spreadsheets, or human operators.

All adversarial testing described in these notes—such as soundness-bypass probes against proof checkers, disguised executable headers, spreadsheet formula payloads, and hidden prompt-override markers—is conducted strictly for defensive verification in isolated local test suites.
